## Chunk Summary

In Settings, "Test it" runs the chosen model over the 31 public worked cases bundled with the app. It uses the same reading call and verifier that real pre-checks use, then says how often the model's fills would have reached the right verdict and the right answer per question. It shows progress ("Case n of 31"), stops early on request, gives each case 60 seconds, and saves the result. The model's label on the description screen then reads "tested — {matches} of 31", with its assumption suffix. It never claims more than that: the 31 cases are public examples, not a guarantee.
