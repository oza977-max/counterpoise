## Chunk Summary

Make the saved record show where each answer came from, and make imported records safe to show. Two parts:

1. **The register's case detail.** When a case carries the new optional `answer_sources`, the case detail shows:
   - one line per distinct read, naming the model and where it ran;
   - the same "N of M answers were filled in by a model and confirmed by you" line as the confirmation screen;
   - per answer, who supplied it ("typed by you", filled from the description and confirmed, changed by you, or from the earlier record), with its quote;
   - "the quote is no longer in your description" when that is true.
2. **The hand-off file.** It carries those sources, validated with strict bounds. Old records and old bundles read exactly as before.

**D1 builds the read side only.** Writing `answer_sources` at confirm, through `toAnswerSources`, is R18-D2's job. Tests use hand-made records that are realistic and shaped exactly as §27.13 says.

## Review Criteria

Expert principles plus BC-001…006, plus these defect classes:
- References;
- Contracts (`AnswerSourceRecord` exactly as §27.13);
- Completeness;
- computed-but-never-consumed fields (the citation lesson: every recorded field must reach the screen or be stated as deliberately unshown).

Attack:
- markup or bidi characters through any shown string;
- prototype pollution through keys;
- an oversized bundle accepted;
- an old bundle refused;
- an old case rendering differently;
- a sentence claiming more than the record holds (the model is "configured", not proven; no digest);
- the register line counts disagreeing with the confirmation screen for the same answers.
