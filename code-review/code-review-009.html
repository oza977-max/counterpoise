5. Important findings (CR9-01 – CR9-02)

CR9-01 — Important
The user guide says a self-approved case is flagged "self-service final"; the app shows it as a normal sign-off

File: docs/user-guide.md:236–238, table row :163 (twins docs/user-guide.html:490, :428); docs/try-these.md:344.

Found by: blind G, calibrated G, blind A, blind D, calibrated D (Important); calibrated A (Minor). 6 panels.

Issue: The guide states "nothing stops a submitter approving their own use case; the record discloses it as self-service final". In the app, a 2LoD approval writes twoloD_reviewed, signedOff becomes true, and the stage note reads "Saved to register — signed off by your AI risk team." The phrase "self-service final" is shown only for a case the policy routes to self-service. Nothing compares submitter and reviewer identity.

Impact: A risk reader or 2LoD reviewer relying on the guide would believe a self-approved case is visibly marked, when it reads exactly like an independent sign-off (only the typed name is labelled "not verified").

Fix: Reword both twins: nothing stops or flags a self-approval; the record shows the sign-off with the typed, unverified name; segregation of duties needs a backend. Correct the table row to "a case the policy routes to self-service". Adjust try-these line 344.

Recommend: fix (docs, both twins).

CR9-02 — Important
"What you need to do: Nothing." under a headline that says to confirm the sign-off first

File: src/components/VerdictDisplay.tsx:480–485 (WhatToDo zero-controls, zero-reviews branch; also the "Then a second-line reviewer signs off" row ~:636).

Found by: blind B (Important, proved), blind C (Minor, proved), calibrated G (Minor), calibrated C (Minor). 4 panels → elevated to Important (it is CR8-02 property P4 on one more surface).

Issue: The branch adds a sign-off caveat only for needsSignOff; signOffMissing and signOffUnknown are never passed in.

Impact: A submitter would read "No sign-off from your AI risk team is on record … confirm with them before you start" (or "We can't tell … whether your AI risk team must sign this off") and directly beneath it "What you need to do — Nothing. This use case sits inside appetite as described…", when a Medium/High case with no controls or reviews has no approving review on record, or at intake before the stage lands.

Evidence: Two probes rendered the real VerdictDisplay with the shipped policy: Medium and High at stage approved, empty trail → headline/next step say confirm, todo says "Nothing."; no stage → "We can't tell…" over "Nothing.". TC-CR8-02a–j assert headline, next steps, who-signs-off and stage note, never the WhatToDo lead.

Fix: Pass the view into WhatToDo; when signOffMissing || signOffUnknown say e.g. "Nothing to put in place — but your AI risk team still needs to confirm the sign-off (see the steps above)."; same for the "Then" row; add render tests for both states and add WhatToDo to the P4 sweep.

Recommend: fix.
