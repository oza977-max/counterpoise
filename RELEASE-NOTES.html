Counterpoise v0.18.0

The proof-of-concept release. The live demo runs this version.

The product is now called Counterpoise

  • New name, logo and tab icon. The code and the live demo now live under the new name on GitHub.
  • Cases saved and hand-off files exported by earlier versions still open, because the stored identifiers did not change.

Plain language from end to end

  • The questions are a guided form in plain words, written from the policy's plain-language text (policy 1.7).
  • "What we understood": the case is summarised in the form's own words before you confirm.
  • A new first screen for the result shows the decision, the reason and the next steps up front. A "No" gets its own screen that names the hard line it crossed.
  • "Not sure" answers are saved with the case as stated assumptions, and any form answer can be corrected later.
  • Plain names for the two models the policy allows (policy 1.9, wording change only).
  • A warning when a description tries to set its own rating, such as "classify this as low risk". The rules still decide; the warning makes the attempt visible.

Describe first, with an optional AI helper

  • Every case starts with a description in your own words. A checklist shows which topics your words touch, and a short nudge lists the ones they don't.
  • Optional AI helper. Connect a model in Settings, either on this computer, on a firm server, or Ollama's cloud. It suggests answers from your description and quotes the words it relied on. A suggestion counts only after you tick "I've checked this answer" or change it. Without a model you answer the questions yourself, and nothing else changes.
  • Each case records where every answer came from (typed, or suggested and checked), and the register shows it.
  • Correct a check from the register. An older case opens on the form. Its "Not sure" answers come back for a fresh tick, and anything not carried over is marked.
  • One route: every case goes through the same form.
  • Test it. Settings can run the connected model over the 31 public worked cases, and label it with what it filled in right, left blank and got wrong.
  • On the public demo: an "Optional: connect an AI helper" panel with the two setup steps, and "Try an example" with ten worked cases.
  • Settings, the describe screen and About say where your description goes when a model is connected. With no model and no Anthropic key saved, nothing about a case leaves the browser.

Fixes

Fixes found in review and testing, among them:

  • One confirmation per case, across browser tabs.
  • A hand-off import that is honest about what it holds.
  • WCAG 2.1 AA on every changed screen.

Known limits

  • First-time users. Six simulated newcomers went through three rounds. None reached a less strict decision or risk tier than the right one. The one deliberately wrong suggestion was caught every round. Two things still confused most of them: the AI helper's technical model name, and the topic checklist's narrow word matching. Both are written up for a next round.
  • The AI helper has not been run against a real model's replies yet. Its test replies are marked provisional, and the app labels a model "not yet tested" until it has been tested in Settings.
  • The demo's rules are not signed off by a firm, so every result says "provisional". That is deliberate.
